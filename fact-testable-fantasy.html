Idea triage protocol · 2 minutes per idea · be honest

Fact / Testable / Fantasy

Every idea you're carrying goes in one of three buckets. Two questions sort it. The rule that makes it work: you're not allowed to lie to yourself about which bucket it's in.

one idea at a time · first action or price tag · park, don't delete

Nothing you write here leaves your device. There's no account, no server, and no analytics on this page — what you type is held in this browser tab so you can reload without losing it, and it is erased the moment you close the tab. "Start over" wipes it immediately. I can't read it. Nobody can.

Fact

Already true — you could verify it right now. Write what it changes, move on.

Testable

First physical action possible today, with what you already have. Not "soon." Not "once I look into it."

Fantasy

Needs something you don't have yet. Not deleted — parked, with the missing pieces named, so it can graduate.

Most people's goals are Fantasy with no prerequisite list. That's why they sit there for years feeling like failure instead of feeling like a shopping list.

Why this one exists

I've had a notes app for years that's just a brain dump. A running list. And here's what's actually different about my life from year to year because of it: the list is longer. That's it. Ideas that got me genuinely excited — solid ones — living in a phone.

If you've got one of those, this sheet is the nudge. Not to organize the list — to move one thing off it into the shared world, where it can do somebody some good. The rest of the page is just the honest way to pick which one.

First — is this a fact, or a plan?

a fact already happened. a plan is something you'd do.

Could you take the first physical action today — with the money, skills, access, and permission you already have?

physical action. a thing done with your hands or your phone, not a thing thought about.

the trap is "basically yes" If the first action needs anything you don't already own — a license, a partner, money, a skill, someone's permission — that's a prerequisite, and this is a Fantasy for now. I run optimistic, so I built this filter to argue with me. An inflated "Testable" is how a week gets busy and moves nothing.
Fact

Good — then it's not an idea, it's information. Information is only worth what it changes. Write what it changes, then stop thinking about it.

Testable

This is the bucket that pays. Two things make it real: the first physical action, and — this is the part everyone skips — what result would prove the idea wrong. If nothing could prove it wrong, you don't have an idea, you have a hope.

Fantasy — for now

This is not a no. Fantasy isn't a no. It's a not-yet with the price tag written on it. Write the missing pieces, specifically — each one is either something you can go get, or the honest reason this idea stays parked.

One of the best things I own sorted Fantasy on a Thursday. I wrote down the four missing pieces — and inside 24 hours a version of the same idea came back Testable, because I could finally see which piece to work around. The list is what does that. The bucket alone does nothing.

The boulder question

Most problems aren't about what you know. They're about who you know. Name one person you could call about this — somebody who'd help push the boulder up the hill in even the smallest way.

Now make the call — today, before the feeling wears off. Whatever bucket the idea landed in, the call is the next best step.
