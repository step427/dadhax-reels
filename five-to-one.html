The five-to-one week

Every one of us has had the argument where no line worked. You hunt for the perfect thing to say in the moment, and the account is already empty.

The fix happens before the hard conversation. Couples who stay stable have about 5 positive moments for every 1 negative during conflict. Those five come from small daily deposits. Pick one person, pick your deposits, and you walk out with a seven-day plan and a tally you tick. Mine is loaded below: the five I talked about on camera.

Nothing you tap or type here leaves your device. No account, no server, no analytics. Your week is erased when you close the tab, so print it or copy it.

not for an unsafe relationship I'm a physical therapist, not a counselor. This is for ordinary relationships that need more deposits. Why this one exists

I read Fight Right by the Gottmans. My takeaway: no trick wins a conflict in the moment. There are practices. That's the hard and fast rule.

The practices are small. A genuine 20-second hug. Hello and goodbye rituals. Genuinely listening. Feeling it with them instead of feeling sorry for them. On any one day, probably nothing. Compounded over time, just like interest in your bank account, it pays dividends. I said it in four reels this week: the 5-to-1 rule, the 20-second hug. This page is the part you do.

Part 1 — Who it's for

One person. A plan for everybody is a plan for nobody.

Part 2 — Your deposits

Tap three to five you'd actually do this week. Small and genuine beats big and rare.

Part 3 — Your week

Your week

    Tap a number when you make that deposit. Tap + on the right when a withdrawal happens: a snap, an eye-roll, the phone while they're talking.

    After a withdrawal: name it ("that came out sharp, I'm sorry"), then go make the next deposit. One negative is repairable. Five deposits is the repair.

    The boulder question

    Name one person who'd do this week with you, on their own person. Now make the call. Ask: “I'm trying five small deposits a day for a week. Want to try it too and compare on Sunday?”

    Where this comes from: the 5-to-1 ratio in conflict, The Gottman Institute. That research is on couples; I use it as a rule of thumb with anyone close. The deposit menu and the tally are my synthesis. Cross out what doesn't fit your person.
