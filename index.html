Who's writing these

I'm Nick. Physical therapist by trade, which means I've spent a decade watching people rebuild things they were told wouldn't come back. Somewhere in there I started buying old houses, then building businesses, then handing the boring parts to machines.

None of it went in a straight line. Most of what I know cost me more than it should have — wrong turns, wasted years, advice I paid for that a free article would have given me.

So I'm compiling it as I go. Not the polished version — the actual one, one sheet at a time, while it's still fresh enough to be honest about what didn't work.

You're the one doing the work here. I'm just the guy a little further up the same road, yelling back about where the potholes are.
