Why this is free

I'm Nick. Physical therapist, ten years — a decade of watching people rebuild things they'd been told wouldn't come back.

Then I bought one old house. My first, and so far my only. It ran three months past what I was quoted, which cost me $2,200 a month I hadn't planned for — $6,600 gone before I'd sold anything. It's under contract now.

That's the whole résumé. I don't own a business. I'm not standing on the far side of this telling you how it's done.

There's an old story about a boy who asks a wise man the secret of happiness. The man hands him a spoon with two drops of oil in it and tells him to walk the whole castle without spilling it. First time around he stares at the spoon and sees nothing. Second time he sees everything and spills the oil.

I'm not the wise man in that story. I'm the kid with the spoon, trying to see the castle and not spill the oil, mostly failing at one or the other. The person I'm best positioned to help is me, five years ago. He didn't need a course. He needed somebody to have written it down. You're about where he was.

The other true thing

I'm not selling anything on this site and I don't plan to. But the whole story here is one old house, so I should say the rest of it plainly:

I'm looking for the next one. Washington or Dakota County, Minnesota. Off-market, or straight from the owner — I'm the buyer, not a middleman passing it along to somebody else.

If that's you, or you know someone quietly thinking about selling and dreading the whole circus of it — signs, showings, strangers walking through the kitchen — my DMs are open. No pressure and no obligation. I'll tell you straight if it isn't a fit for me, and if I know somebody better suited I'll point you at them.

What I'll do

Show up every day and put something out — a few short videos, one new tool a week, whatever I learned that's worth passing on. All of it free. No email to read any of it, and nothing good sitting behind a gate. I don't know exactly what this becomes. I know I'll keep showing up.

What I ask

Nothing here costs money and nothing sits behind an email. If you want to call something the price, it's this: when one of these is useful, send it to one or two people who'd use it too. That's the whole exchange. I'm trusting you with it, you pass it on. Nobody has to collect anything.

And if you want the next one before anyone else, I'm building an interest list. Here's exactly what that means, because I didn't know either:

You type your email. You get one message confirming it worked. Then nothing until there's a new tool — about one a week — and when it comes, the email is the tool. Not a teaser for the tool.

What most lists cost you:

  • Your address sold or rented to whoever pays for it
  • A "welcome sequence" of seven emails you never asked for
  • The free thing turning out to be a door into a paid thing
  • Unsubscribe buried in grey six-point type

What this one costs you: nothing. I'm not selling anything. If that ever changes I'll say so in plain words before it does, not after. One click to leave, and I won't email you to ask why.

The list itself isn't open yet — I'm still setting it up, and I'd rather say that than put up a box that doesn't work. The ask above stands either way, and it's the one that matters. If you want to tell me what to build next, my DMs are open.
