Why this is free

I'm Nick. Physical therapist, ten years — a decade of watching people rebuild things they'd been told wouldn't come back.

Then I bought one old house. My first, and so far my only. It ran three months past what I was quoted, which cost me $2,200 a month I hadn't planned for — $6,600 gone before I'd sold anything. It's under contract now.

That's the whole résumé. I don't own a business. I'm not standing on the far side of this telling you how it's done.

The person I'm best positioned to help is me, five years ago. He didn't need a course. He needed somebody to have written it down. You're about where he was.

What I'll do

Show up every day and put something out — a few short videos, one new tool a week, whatever I learned that's worth passing on. All of it free. No email to read any of it, and nothing good sitting behind a gate. I don't know exactly what this becomes. I know I'll keep showing up.
