2026-09-01 Pain is a signal

Watch it on Instagram →
This one only exists there — Instagram stopped letting sites embed reels.

Something happened this year that hurt, and I caught myself wanting to file it under "bad" and walk away.

Here's where I landed instead: that pain is a signal.

Not a verdict. Not a punishment. A signal - the same way a smoke alarm isn't the fire.

It's telling me something is out of alignment. And the move isn't to sit in it, and it isn't to numb it. The move is action. Focused action, in one specific direction, like a laser.

Scattered action is just anxiety with a to-do list.

So when it hurts this week, ask the alarm what it's pointing at. Then go do that one thing.

2026-09-01 Quit calling it good or bad

Watch it on Instagram →
This one only exists there — Instagram stopped letting sites embed reels.

I'm growing to hate "good" and "bad." Not the words - the habit.

Because both of them are judgments, and honestly? I am not well equipped to pass judgment. Neither is anybody I've met. We call a thing bad on Tuesday and thank God for it by Christmas.

So I swapped the question.

Instead of "was that good or bad," I ask: is this in alignment with what I want to get done in this life?

That question has an answer. The other one just has an argument.

And when the answer is no, I don't have to hate it. I just don't have to carry it either.

Try it on the thing that's been sitting on your chest all week. Not good, not bad. Aligned, or not.

There's one of these every day. You're either getting them or you're not.
