2026-10-01 New jointer: don't pull all 4 screws

Watch it on Instagram →
This one only exists there — Instagram stopped letting sites embed reels.

New jointer, dust port goes on last. Most of us back out all four screws.

You don't have to. Loosen two, top and bottom. Remove the other two. The port slides in, and the top of it angles down steeper toward the hose. Re-thread, snug them up, done.

Learned this setting up my own tools. Follow and catch the next one.

2026-09-29 One 45° cut hangs 90% of my garage

Watch it on Instagram →
This one only exists there — Instagram stopped letting sites embed reels.

A French cleat is one strip of wood ripped at 45 degrees. Half goes on the wall. The other half goes on the back of whatever you're hanging. They hook together, and you can pick it up and move it anywhere.

About 90% of my garage hangs on one.

The cleat is the easy part. The cut list is where the Saturday goes: how many rails, how many 45s, how many sheets.

There's a free tool for that now. Before you buy a sheet, measure your wall once and type in the width. A 10-foot wall comes back as 9 rails, twelve 45° rips and 2 sheets, with every joint landing on a stud.

It's in my profile.

2026-09-28 Check it before you trust it

Watch it on Instagram →
This one only exists there — Instagram stopped letting sites embed reels.

A new tool comes out of the box and most of us start cutting.

Put a square on it first. My jointer's fence stops are set at the factory to 90 and 135, and I checked the 90 with my speed square. Perfect out of the box. If yours isn't, check your manual for the stop adjustment.

135 is just a 45 from the other side, the same angle every cleat on my wall is cut at.

Saves you a ruined board. Follow and the next one's already in your feed.
