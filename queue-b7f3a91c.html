Mon Aug 101:00pJust-in-case people vs just-in-time people 
Hey Dad,

There are just-in-case people and there are just-in-time people.

Just-in-case waits until he's sure. One more book, one more course, one more year of getting ready — waiting to become the guy who'd be qualified to start.

Just-in-time moves, and works it out on the way down.

I was just-in-case for a long time and I called it being responsible. It wasn't. It was fear with a respectable job title.

Here's the part nobody mentions: the certainty you're waiting on doesn't show up before the action. It shows up because of it.

Wrong action, messy action, or a slam dunk — matters far less than moving. You can steer something that's rolling. You can't steer something parked.
