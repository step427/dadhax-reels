Fri Sep 181:00prepostJoint boards without a jointer 
Hey Dad,

No jointer? You don't need one for a tight glue-up.

I tried three ways:
1. Table saw straight-edge jig. Took a lot of material off, but left the edge wavy.
2. Handheld power planer. Gaps along the joint, worst at the ends.
3. Router with a flush-trim bit riding a straight edge. Tight joints, just needs sanding.

Hands down the easiest was the router. For a big project with a lot of jointing, a cheap jointer will still save you time.

A glue-up is a good third thing: two sets of hands, one clamp job.

All three methods, and every joint up close, are in the full video on my YouTube (NicksDadHax).
