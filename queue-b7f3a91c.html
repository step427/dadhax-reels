Mon Aug 171:00pWillpower is finite — the Swiss cheese model 
Hey Dad,

The gift bags called me out. There WAS a system — and it's broken, because it ran on willpower.

A system that relies on willpower is bound to break. Willpower is finite. One bad day, one bad week, and the energy that was holding the whole thing together is gone.

So you don't build one perfect layer. You build layers, plural. In medicine it's called the Swiss cheese model: every layer has holes, but you stack them so the holes don't line up. More layers, fewer things get through — and when something breaks, it's an easy fix instead of a collapse.

Stop blaming your discipline. Start stacking layers.
