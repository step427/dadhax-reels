Wed Oct 281:00prepostStripped drawer screw: toothpicks + glue (re-cut) 
Hey Dad,

You can fix a drawer screw that just spins in its hole with a couple of toothpicks and a little wood glue. No new hardware.

1. Find the stripped hole. The screw that spins and never bites is your problem.
2. Glue the toothpicks and push them in until the hole is packed tight.
3. Trim them flush with a utility knife.
4. Drive the screw by hand, not the drill, so you don't strip the fresh wood again. Stop when it's snug.

This dresser is solid pine. In particleboard, toothpicks are a weaker fix.

No need to wait on the glue. It sets up in there while the drawer is back in use.

I post the fix I wish somebody had told me, every day. Follow and it's free.
