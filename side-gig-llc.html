Why this one exists

Once upon a time a good boy followed the prescribed path — school, degree, career — and excelled at nearly every stop. Then one day: his own family, gray hairs coming in, a life not yet lived nagging at him. No idea where to begin. So he took the one next step that inspired him — before he knew what value he'd give the world, he opened his first LLC. That was me. The filing wasn't a business. It was the decision, finally made somewhere that could hold one.

It makes it real

A filed thing with a name is a decision you can't quietly un-make. You stop auditioning and start operating.

It separates the money

Its own bank account from day one. Year-end becomes a report, not archaeology through your personal statements.

It's built to hold risk

Designed so the gig's problems stay the gig's. How much protection you really get depends on keeping it clean — an attorney question, not a web page's.

It's cheap and reversible

Most states: $50–$300 to open, a form to close. The price of ending "someday" is a dinner out.

the honest rail Not a lawyer, not an accountant. Whether an LLC fits you — taxes, elections, liability in your state — goes to a pro. This page gets you to the starting line; it doesn't replace them.
