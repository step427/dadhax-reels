The Toolbox

Every tool in here serves one job: the hard work that makes a dad's life powerful. Purpose, habits, honest thinking, the first real move. Start anywhere — each one hands you the next.

How to use it: pick the one that names the thing you're stuck on. Give it the ten minutes it asks for. Every tool ends with a next step you can take tonight — none of them end with "buy something."
