Your website is a public vision board.

Most small-business sites are somebody else's guess: a template, stock photos, words that could belong to anyone. You already see yours in your head. Five questions and a few pictures, and I build from what you see instead of what I assume.

A nurse practitioner opening her own clinic sent me a few lines on why she was doing it and one AI picture of the clinic she pictured. That was enough to build a site that felt like hers on the first pass.

1 of 5 · the only one that's required

The real reason. What happened that made you start it? Easiest way: tap the mic on your phone's keyboard and just talk.

2 of 5

Picture your best customer the day they go looking for you. What's going on in their life?

3 of 5

Three words is plenty. If there's a website you love (any business, any industry), paste the link and say what you love about it.

4 of 5 · optional

Show me what you see.

Screenshots of sites you like, an AI picture of your vision, photos of your space or your work, your logo. Up to 6.

No picture yet? Make one in two minutes

Paste this into ChatGPT or Gemini, fill in the brackets, and upload what it makes:

Make a realistic photo of [my business: a home care agency / a clinic / a shop] on its best day, the way I picture it a year from now. The feeling should be [three words]. Show [the space, the people, the work]. No text or logos in the image.

5 of 5

When a new customer finds you, what should they do first?

And where do they find you today?

Where do I send your first look?

This goes to me and nobody else. Your answers sit in my private spreadsheet and your pictures in my email. Nothing is sold or shared, and if you ask, I delete it. Health and care businesses: please leave out any patient or client details.

  1. I read every answer and look at every picture.

  3. You tell me what's off, and we fix it until it looks like what you see.
